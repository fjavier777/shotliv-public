# Shotliv — Product Value Proposition & Differentiators

**Product:** Shotliv  
**Positioning:** Better Photos. Effortlessly.  
**Core concept:** Enhance. Don't Reinvent.  
**Document purpose:** Public product reference for positioning, product decisions, and marketing communication.

---

## 1. Product overview

Shotliv is a lightweight image-enhancement tool designed to make everyday photo adjustments fast, simple, private, and accessible.

Delivered as a Google Chrome extension, Shotliv processes selected images locally in the browser. Its initial experience focuses on essential adjustments and a straightforward workflow rather than the complexity of professional editing software or the unpredictability of generative image tools.

Shotliv serves a general audience. Its priority professional use cases are vehicle listings and real-estate photography, where users often need to prepare multiple images repeatedly. The product remains useful for personal photographs, online sellers, and other everyday image workflows.

Shotliv is not intended to replace professional photo-editing suites or compete with AI image-generation products. Its purpose is to solve practical image-enhancement tasks with fewer steps and less friction.

## 2. The three product pillars

### 2.1 Simplicity and productivity
Make image enhancement accessible and reduce the time and effort required to complete everyday editing tasks.

### 2.2 Privacy and user control
Process selected images locally in the browser, reducing exposure associated with uploading photos to external processing services.

### 2.3 Preservation and efficiency
Enhance existing photographs through conventional image-processing operations rather than generative reconstruction, without consuming generative AI tokens or credits.

## 3. Core differentiators

### 3.1 Fast, low-friction workflow
Shotliv is available directly in the browser. Users do not need to open a separate desktop editor or visit an online image-processing service to perform supported adjustments.

**Benefits**
- Fewer steps between choosing an image and editing it.
- Quick access from the browser.
- Local processing without an image-upload step.
- Less interruption to the user's existing workflow.

**Marketing message:**  
"Enhance your photos in seconds, without the hassle."

### 3.2 Simple and accessible experience
Shotliv focuses on essential operations instead of presenting users with the complexity of a full professional editing suite.

**Benefits**
- A focused interface.
- A short learning curve.
- Straightforward editing operations.
- Useful results without requiring advanced editing skills.

**Marketing message:**  
"Better photos without becoming an editing expert."

### 3.3 Local image processing
Selected images are processed locally in the user's browser. The current editing workflow does not need to upload image files to Shotliv servers or external image-processing services.

**Benefits**
- Greater control over selected files.
- No image upload for supported local editing.
- Reduced reliance on external processing services.
- No need for a cloud image-processing backend for core local operations.

**Marketing message:**  
"Your photos stay on your device. Processing happens locally."

### 3.4 Privacy and confidentiality by design
Local processing reduces the need to share personal or professional photographs with external services just to perform basic image adjustments.

The current privacy policy states that selected image files, image contents, and filenames are not transmitted to the developer or to Shotliv servers, and are not shared with third parties.

**Benefits**
- Less exposure of private images to external services.
- Greater control over image files.
- No upload of selected images for processing.
- A workflow that can be more suitable for users handling client photographs.

**Marketing message:**  
"Your images are yours. We don't need to receive them to enhance them."

Avoid absolute claims such as "100% secure" or "total confidentiality." Local processing reduces specific data-transfer risks but does not eliminate risks associated with the user's device, browser, operating system, or other software.

### 3.5 Offline potential
Because the current image-processing workflow runs locally in the browser, Shotliv may allow supported editing operations to work without an internet connection once the extension is installed.

**Validation status:** Offline operation must be verified with the installed extension while the browser is disconnected. Test interface loading, supported operations, preview, and export before advertising offline support as a confirmed feature.

### 3.6 Non-generative enhancement and photo preservation
Shotliv uses conventional image-processing operations rather than generative AI to reconstruct or reinterpret the photographed scene.

The goal is to adjust the existing image, not generate a new interpretation of the scene. Conventional adjustments can still change image properties such as brightness, contrast, saturation, and color values; therefore, do not promise that every pixel or visual characteristic remains unchanged.

**Marketing message:**  
"Improve your photo. Don't reinvent it."

### 3.7 No generative AI tokens or credits
The current image-enhancement workflow does not rely on generative AI APIs or cloud inference. Supported local operations do not consume generative AI tokens or credits.

This does not mean Shotliv has no product usage limits, subscription limits, or other commercial restrictions. Those are separate from AI-provider token or credit consumption.

**Marketing message:**  
"No AI tokens. No generative credits. Just image enhancement."

### 3.8 Reduced dependence on cloud processing infrastructure
The current local workflow does not require a remote image-processing server.

**Benefits**
- No remote processing queue for core local operations.
- No image upload bandwidth required for processing.
- Reduced dependence on external processing availability.
- Less cloud-processing infrastructure required for the current workflow.

**Marketing message:**  
"Your device does the work. No cloud processing required."

### 3.9 Predictable, controlled adjustments
Shotliv's conventional image-processing operations follow defined algorithms and user-selected parameters, rather than generating a new interpretation of the image.

**Benefits**
- Parameter-driven adjustments.
- Repeatable settings when the same inputs and parameters are used.
- Direct user control over supported settings.
- A clear relationship between selected adjustments and the resulting image.

### 3.10 Batch processing
**Status: Planned; not currently available.**

Batch processing is the leading candidate for the first Pro capability. It is intended to let users select multiple images and apply supported operations in a more efficient local workflow.

**Expected benefits**
- Less repetitive work.
- Time savings when preparing many images.
- More consistent application of adjustments.
- Greater productivity for recurring image workflows.

**Priority use cases**
- Vehicle listings and dealership inventory.
- Real-estate listings and property portfolios.
- Product catalogs and other recurring commercial workflows.

Do not advertise batch processing as available until it has been implemented, tested, and released.

## 6. Free + Pro product model

Shotliv is being developed around two access levels.

### Free
- Core image-enhancement tools.
- One image per editing operation.
- Unlimited use over time.
- Local processing.
- No mandatory account in the initial Free experience.

The intention is to keep Free genuinely useful. Artificial daily, monthly, or lifetime photo quotas are not part of the current product direction.

### Pro
A paid subscription focused on productivity for users with recurring image-preparation needs. Batch processing is the leading candidate for the first paid differentiator.

Additional Pro features must be based on validated demand. A single Pro plan is intended to serve both vehicle and real-estate workflows; separate industry subscriptions are not currently planned.

Pricing and subscription terms will be validated before launch. No price is presented here as a confirmed offer.

## 7. Privacy and the future account service

Local image processing and account licensing are separate concerns.

If a Pro subscription is introduced, a minimal account service may handle authentication, checkout, subscription status, and feature entitlements. It must not receive, store, or process user photographs.

The extension should continue processing images locally. Payment credentials and privileged service secrets must remain outside the extension.

Any future account, payment, analytics, or network functionality must be reflected in the privacy policy and Chrome Web Store disclosures before release. Do not claim that the future subscription service collects no personal data; account and billing data may be necessary to provide paid access.

## 8. Marketing and communication principles

Marketing should lead with user benefits rather than technical implementation details.

1. **Speed and simplicity:** complete common image-enhancement tasks without complex software.
2. **Local processing:** explain that selected images are processed on the device and are not uploaded for editing.
3. **Preservation:** distinguish conventional enhancement from generative scene reconstruction.
4. **Professional productivity:** promote batch processing only after implementation and release.
5. **Honest availability:** clearly distinguish current features, planned capabilities, and validated claims.

Avoid absolute claims such as "100% secure" or unverified claims about offline operation, performance, or batch limits.

## 10. Technical validation before public claims

1. **Offline operation:** test extension startup, editing, preview, and export while disconnected.
2. **Network behavior:** monitor requests and verify that selected images and associated image data are not transmitted.
3. **Local processing:** confirm every advertised operation executes on the device.
4. **Image preservation:** compare input and output and document which properties may change.
5. **Predictability:** repeat operations with identical inputs and parameters and compare outputs.
6. **No generative dependency:** review dependencies and network calls.
7. **Batch processing:** document implementation, performance, and supported formats before announcing availability.
8. **Subscription data separation:** verify that account and entitlement requests do not transmit image files.

## 11. Product principles

- Simplicity before complexity.
- Speed before feature overload.
- Privacy through architecture.
- User control before automatic generation.
- Validated utility before unnecessary capabilities.
- Professional productivity without excluding general users.
- Clear communication about what is available, planned, and verified.

**Shotliv does not need to do everything. It needs to do its job exceptionally well.**
